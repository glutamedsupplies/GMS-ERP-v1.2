Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GMS ERP collects, uses, and protects information when you access our web and desktop applications. If you are using GMS ERP through your employer or organization, your administrator may control your account and data.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, role, and company identifier.
  • Work activity data such as attendance logs, time in/out records, and role assignments.
  • Operational data such as sales orders, inventory items, and customer requests you submit.
  • Technical data such as IP address, device identifiers, browser type, and basic usage logs.

How we use information

  • Provide and maintain the GMS ERP service and its features.
  • Authenticate users, enforce role-based access, and secure the platform.
  • Generate reports and operational insights for authorized company users.
  • Improve performance and reliability of the app.

Sharing and disclosure

We share information only as needed to operate the service, such as with hosting, authentication, and infrastructure providers, or when required by law. We do not provide your personal data to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data retention

We retain data for as long as your organization maintains its account or as needed to meet legal and operational requirements. Administrators may manage retention within their organization's policies.

Security

We apply technical and organizational measures to protect data, including access controls and encryption in transit where supported. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

Your choices

You can request access, updates, or deletion of your data through your organization's administrator. If you are an administrator, you can manage users and permissions within the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact your account administrator or email support@gmserp.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product or legal changes. When we make updates, we will revise the date above.
