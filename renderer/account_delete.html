Account Deletion

Remove your GMS ERP account with a verified code.

Use this page from the app or from any browser if the app is already uninstalled. We send a one-time code to your verified login or Google email before the account is removed.

What gets removed

Your login access, saved profile record, and active app session for this GMS ERP account.

What may stay

Operational records that the business still needs to keep for reporting, accounting, payroll, or legal retention.

Important for head admins

You need another active company admin first before deleting the last admin account for a company workspace.

Public deletion flow

Confirm permanent account deletion

Enter your company ID and verified email to receive an account deletion code. If you are already signed in, we can prefill the right account details automatically.

Browser-only recovery path

This page is intended to stay usable even when the mobile app is no longer installed, so you can still request and confirm deletion from any browser.

Need to connect an email first?

If your account does not have a verified login or Google email yet, sign in first and connect one from Settings before using this page.
